Questions before we start

Which platform should I choose?

Shopify suits stores that want to sell and scale. Wix works well for brand and service websites you want to manage easily. WordPress and custom PHP & Laravel fit content-heavy sites and tailored features. Tell me your goal and I’ll recommend one.

Can you improve my existing store?

Yes. I customize themes, add sections, fix layout issues, and optimize speed on stores that are already live.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and the number of custom features. After we talk, I share a clear timeline before any work begins.
